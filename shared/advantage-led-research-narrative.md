# Advantage-Led Research Narrative

Organize author-side research and writing around the strongest honest contribution.
This governs presentation of evidence, not exploration methodology. Scientific
decisions follow the project's `SCIENTIFIC_EXPLORATION_PROTOCOL.md` under
`AGENTS.md`. Apply this narrative guide when writing is in scope; it does not
require an exploratory project to produce a paper.

## Research Continuity

Reuse existing question cards, claim/experiment IDs and story handoffs. In a
minimal-paper project, narrative belongs in `paper/writing_outline.md`, facts in
`paper/methods_results.md`, settings in the active configuration. Update only
affected fields; do not create another ledger or a full card for a local edit.

| Preserve when relevant | Content |
|---|---|
| Question, contribution, fair arena | Leading advantage; state `candidate`, `supported` or `needs-reframing`; task/comparator/metrics/population/scale/budget |
| Claims and evidence | Existing C#/E# IDs, result locator/version, retellable sentence and changed dependencies |
| Constraints and decisions | Word/venue/format limits, prohibited or declined work, user decision locator and artifact/version/scope |
| Next authorized action | Existing approvals, pending approvals and next in-scope step |
| Excluded evidence | Source key/version, exclusion reason and verification locator, affected claims and any verified correction |

Evidence states are not authorization, task completion or reproducibility
verdicts; preserve separate story and material-verification statuses.
Name evidence that could support or defeat a planned claim; retain actual
conditions, skipped work and consequential contrary results. Reconcile changed
configuration/results before reusing claims. A changed advantage remains
`needs-reframing` until revised scope is supported.

Continue only within the requested scope: run-only ends with results; an
authorized research-to-paper task continues downstream. Reuse unchanged explicit
choices without reapproval. A declined optional change stays declined; a new
validity defect still needs resolution. Do not convert declined suggestions into
limitations or give author preferences to independent reviewers to suppress findings.

Keep excluded sources out of downstream prose, even if old bibliographies retain
them. Distinguish nonexistent/disproven evidence, correctable metadata and
access-only notes. Restore only after resolving the exclusion and reverifying
the claim, with any applicable approval; never restore silently from an old draft.

## 1. Lead With the Advantage

Choose one contribution: new capability, clearer mechanism, cost/data savings,
scalability/transfer/use value, credible incremental evidence or an informative
hypothesis/method boundary. State precisely what changes and for whom.
Supporting contributions serve that point; categorical uniqueness is not required.

## 2. Choose the Winning Arena

Define a useful, fair task, comparator, metric, population and setting.
Comprehensive dominance is unnecessary. Declared changes may follow the research
purpose, but must not hide failed primary outcomes, omit required endpoints,
remove relevant simple baselines or make comparison empty. A metric discovered
after results is exploratory, not a silent replacement of the primary metric.
A claimed cost, deployment or interpretability trade-off needs evidence of both
cost and benefit; do not invent advantages to explain a lower score.

## 3. Carry the Advantage Through the Paper

- Abstract/Introduction: problem -> gap -> approach -> strongest supported result
  -> significance. Detailed analysis belongs in Results; paragraph quotas and
  roadmaps are optional unless required.
- Results: what readers must believe, in argument order.
- Discussion: what the evidence adds, why and where it matters.
- Conclusion: core contribution, decisive evidence, memorable implication.
  Add no new defect, experiment or unsupported claim. Repeat a boundary only
  when omission misleads or reporting requires it; no limitations quota.

Place material boundaries near the claims they qualify; imagined reviewer
attacks do not deserve prominence by default.

## 4. Give Every Analysis One Argument Job

Each prominent experiment/model/figure/table must establish, explain, demonstrate,
distinguish or bound the contribution. Reuse an evidence/job map when choosing
analyses or structuring a paper; it is not a mandatory output for local prose edits.
Add robustness, sensitivity, subgroups or ablations only for a named uncertainty.
Omitting/demoting presentation does not delete raw data, run records, required
endpoints or necessary controls. Keep factual execution history intact.

## 5. Do Not Expand the Paper's Burden of Proof

Include a caveat when it changes truth/scope/interpretation, is required reporting,
or prevents a material overclaim. Otherwise omit it from the main narrative.
Replace emotional or self-undermining comparisons with specific observations
and evidenced trade-offs. Apply this semantically: `遗憾的是` is usually rhetorical;
`only 10 samples` or `仅需一种传感器` may convey essential information.
Do not mechanically delete qualifiers or useful limitations.

## 6. Write the Result Logic, Not the Project Diary

Reorder the paper around the conclusion the evidence supports. Changed questions
or comparisons retain primary outcomes and identify post hoc analysis; material
scientific changes follow existing authorization. Use chronology only when the
discovery process itself is evidence.

## Revision Invariants

Preserve numbers/units, direction/negation, comparison conditions, uncertainty,
causal meaning, population/time/scale and citation ownership. A cited background
finding must not become this study's finding. Preserve supported strength in
both directions; use evidence, not a universal verb ranking.

Ordinary reordering/emphasis/qualifier removal needs no sentence-level approval.
Surface material scientific changes under existing rules. Unsupported factual
assertions need support/attribution, omission if nonessential, or a material-gap
note; adding `may` supplies no evidence.

When results or claim scope change, reconcile all abstract languages, title and
conclusion with the current body/evidence. If a dependent section is outside
editing scope, identify its stale claim and prepare the correction within scope.

## Integrity Floor

Never hide evidence that overturns, reverses or materially narrows the central
claim. Preserve preregistered/required outcomes, fair comparators, uncertainty
and material design limitations. Distinguish necessary boundaries from
speculative reviewer attacks; surface the latter only when they affect a decision.
