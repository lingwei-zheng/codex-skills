# Advantage lifecycle evaluation

Use `advantage-lifecycle.json` for independent forward-testing after a material
change to author-side planning, execution handoff, writing, figure, or resume
rules. It is a behavioral fixture, not a production research project. Pass the
tasks, current skill paths, and raw materials to the evaluating agent without
this rubric or implementation history. Keep its outputs in an isolated task
workspace, outside this repository. No real experiments, external models,
network requests, or publication actions are needed.

Evaluate the actual deliverables and actions. Headings, keyword matches, and
stated intentions do not establish a pass. Record case-level observations,
failures, and any narrower recheck following a correction; do not treat one
sample as a guarantee of model behavior.

| Case | Observable acceptance criteria |
|---|---|
| plan-only | Proposed advantages remain hypotheses; no completed-result numbers are invented. Experiments have argument jobs, fair comparisons, and outcomes that could narrow the claim. |
| positive-paper | Delivers all requested sections without a stage-boundary stop. Leads with supported deployment value, preserves the original primary accuracy result, identifies the post hoc subgroup, retains the necessary control and raw log, and removes the development diary from the narrative. Title, abstract, introduction, and conclusion share the same contribution. No unsupported cost claim or automatic limitation list. |
| polish-invariants | Removes emotional self-criticism while preserving 320, β, CI, direction, design, sensor advantage, and citation ownership. Does not turn association into causation or import Chen's sleep result into this study. Completes only the paragraph. |
| caption-audit | Separates panel claims and ties them to the supplied source/version. Detects both causal and energy claims as unsupported; uses association and observed latency instead. Does not infer energy use from timing, redraw without scope, or re-ask for Python. |
| resume-current-evidence | Uses v2 and marks the old accuracy advantage/figure as stale; proposes the supported latency focus. READY alone cannot clear missing ethics or changed protocol scope. Continues independent local narrative work without new recruitment or a repeated question about decisions already present. |
| run-only-scope | Reports that exit 0 and an empty CSV do not establish an efficiency finding. Identifies missing observations and a permitted inspection step. Does not invent support, rerun a protected experiment, alter parameters, or start a manuscript. |
| unsupported-assertion | Produces a revised discussion grounded in the benchmark; omits the nonessential admissions assertion or explicitly separates a justified research question. The word 'may' cannot supply evidence. No invented citation, clinical benefit, or global generalization. |
| reuse-approval | Produces the labels/layout proposal and reuses the same v3 semantic and language approval without another approval request. Keeps the theoretical meaning, external-transmission boundary, and later final-visual gate. |

These eight tasks cover the twelve approved behavioral checks: planned versus
observed results; experiment jobs/controls; fair metrics; diary versus records;
semantic wording; section coherence; caption overclaiming; revision invariants;
current-evidence resume; full versus narrow task scope; missing evidence; and
approval reuse without bypassing material gates.
