# Space-Time Constraints and Greenspace Terminology

Use this calibration reference when the project explicitly adopts the canonical
terms below. Do not impose them on unrelated papers or replace an established
term in another field without checking that project's own terminology contract.

## Canonical labels

| Concept | Canonical label | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| The combined construct family | `Space-Time Constraints` | Preserve capitalization, plural form, and hyphenation. Do not silently rename it to a generic “mobility pattern” or an unvalidated latent score. |
| Vegetation-cover exposure measure | `greenspace` | Write as one word. Do not use `green space` or `green-space` for this measure. |
| Time-related constraint | `temporal fixity` | Use the short figure/table label after the first operational definition. |
| Place-related constraint | `spatial fixity` | Use the short figure/table label after the first operational definition. |
| Shared-obligation constraint | `coupling burden` | Do not expand it to general social interaction. |
| Repeated-anchor constraint | `anchor concentration` | Do not equate it with the whole activity space or complete spatial restriction. |
| Cross-day routine constraint | `schedule regularity` | Do not equate it with a full 24-hour routine or destination repetition alone. |

## Daytime exposure labels

When the exposure is computed only from the defined daytime window, use the full
labels on first occurrence:

- `daytime mobility-based greenspace exposure estimate`
- `daytime home-time fraction`
- `daytime out-of-home greenspace exposure estimate`

After definition, the shorter labels `daytime mobility-based exposure`,
`daytime home-time`, and `daytime out-of-home exposure` are acceptable when the
referent is unambiguous. Do not remove `daytime` from a first occurrence or imply
that the measure represents a complete 24-hour exposure.

## Measurement and writing boundary

- State the actual daytime window (for this project, HKT `[06:00,18:00)`) in the
  Methods section and when a scope ambiguity would matter.
- Compute home-time and out-of-home exposure from the same daytime dynamic GPS
  exposure stream after the nighttime interval has been excluded; describe them
  as daytime pathway variables, not as all-day exposure measures.
- Explain the calculation at first definition; use the canonical short labels in
  tables, figures, and later prose.
- Keep `residential greenspace exposure` distinct from the daytime mobility-based
  estimate. Neither is automatically a gold-standard or “true exposure” measure.
- Keep observational association language separate from causal, mediation, or
  mechanism claims unless the design supports those claims.
